Projects

Digital poka-yoke for process step tracking

Internship project, Termokar, 2026

In heat exchanger production, brazing, solvent cleaning and nitrogen purging were sometimes skipped or incomplete. I wrote a Python console application that enforces the correct sequence and quantities.

  • The operator enters the order quantity and product type. A step counts as complete only when the entered quantity reaches the order quantity.
  • Cleaning cannot start before brazing is complete, and purging cannot start before cleaning is complete.
  • Shipment is approved only when all steps are complete.

This is a prototype. It was not deployed on the shop floor, and its effect on scrap and rework rates was not measured. Barcode or sensor input and ERP integration are possible next steps.

Statistical process control in wheel manufacturing

IE 3513 Quality Assurance and Reliability, term project, Fall 2025-2026

A case study on customer complaints about wheel thickness, with a target of 250 mm. I built X̄-R control charts from 50 subgroups of Machine A.

  • The first charts showed points outside the control limits. The R chart showed no abnormal variation, so the problem was in the mean. After the assignable causes were identified and the affected subgroups removed, the process was stable.
  • Showed that combining Machines A, B and C on one chart violates rational subgrouping and hides real problems.
  • Analyzed separately, the means of B and C were off target.
  • Compared two improvement options, reducing variation and re-centering the mean, and chose re-centering because it raised Cpk more.
